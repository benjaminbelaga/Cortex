The upstream reference used for reconciliation is
`80de4330ede5a7d1ea71456e54d26473cbd50c99`. Its public Git history preserves the
individual upstream contributions. This repository begins with a clean-history
export; the previous private development history remains private.

Cortex adds independent account monitoring, verified enrolment, explicit source
selection, session and usage views, and native provider integrations. Third-party
packages retain their own licenses and attribution in their source distributions.
